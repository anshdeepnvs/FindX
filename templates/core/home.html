How It Works

Why Scammers Cannot Cheat Our System

Traditional lost-and-found boards reveal everything, allowing scammers to claim items effortlessly. Here is how our 3-tier protection defends real owners:

🔒
Step 1

Hidden Identifying Markers

When someone finds an item, they log secret identifying details (lockscreen wallpaper, internal pocket items, scratches, partial serial) that are strictly hidden from the public.

✍️
Step 2

Blind Challenge Verification

A claimant must answer the finder's blind challenge questions without seeing the answers in advance. Imposters who guess blindly are immediately flagged.

🤖
Step 3

AI Scoring & Safe Handover OTP

Our AI compares the claimant's answers against the finder's private notes and provides a 0-100% confidence score. Approved owners receive a one-time Handover PIN for safe exchange.
