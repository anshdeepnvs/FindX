Why Traditional Lost & Found Fails,
And How FindX Solves It

Standard boards publish everything—allowing imposters to call and fake ownership. FindX uses a 3-tier cryptographic shield.

1

Hidden Vault Details

Finders record secret identifying clues (e.g. lockscreen wallpaper, internal pocket items, scratches, partial serial number). These are never visible to the public.

2

Blind Challenge & AI Evaluation

Claimants must answer verification questions blind. Our Gemini AI and sentence embedding engine analyze whether their answers genuinely match the finder's encrypted vault notes.

3

6-Digit Physical Handover OTP

Once the finder accepts the owner, direct chat unlocks and a secret 6-digit Handover OTP is generated. The finder only releases the item after verifying the OTP in person!

🤝 Safe Public Meeting Handshake

Coordinate a public handover right in FindX chat. Inspect the item, verify the 6-digit OTP, and mark returned safely.
