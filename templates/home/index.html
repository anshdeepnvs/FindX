How FindX Works

Intelligent matching meets airtight anti-scam security.

Unlike ordinary bulletin boards, FindX never reveals private identifying marks or contact numbers publicly.

1

Report with Blind Details

Report your lost or found item. Secret marks, wallpapers, or contents are stored in an encrypted vault and never shown to the public.

2

Automated AI Matching

Our sentence-embedding and visual analysis engine continuously correlates lost and found reports by semantic context, date proximity, and category.

3

Verified Return & OTP

The owner answers the blind verification challenge. Once approved by the finder, secure in-platform chat unlocks with a 6-digit physical handover OTP.

⚖️ Core Product Principle

"AI Finds Potential Matches.
Ownership Verification Confirms The Real Owner."

We never claim AI alone proves ownership. Security questions and physical handover OTPs guarantee the rightful owner recovers their belongings.
